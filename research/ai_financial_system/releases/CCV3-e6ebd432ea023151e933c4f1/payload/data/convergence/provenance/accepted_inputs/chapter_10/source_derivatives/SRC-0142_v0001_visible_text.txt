WARRANT AND COMMON STOCK AGREEMENT dated as of August 22, 2025 (this “
Agreement
”), between INTEL CORPORATION, a corporation organized under the laws of the State of Delaware (the “
Company
”), and the UNITED STATES DEPARTMENT OF COMMERCE (“
Commerce
”).
WHEREAS, the Company and Commerce are party to that certain Direct Funding Agreement, dated as of November 25, 2024 (the “
DFA
”);
WHEREAS, the Company has requested that Commerce make a total of
$8,869,800,000.00 of disbursements, consisting of (1) the acceleration of certain disbursements under the DFA in the amount of $5,695,000,000.00 (the “
Acceleration
”) and (2) $3,174,800,000.00 of disbursements in respect of Secure Enclave (the “
Secure Enclave
Disbursements
” and, together with the Acceleration, the “
Disbursements
”);
WHEREAS, as appropriate compensation to the Federal Government of the United States of America (the “
US Government
”) for, and as a condition to Commerce’s willingness to permit, the Disbursements, the Company has agreed to issue to Commerce in a private placement (1) warrants to purchase 240,516,150 shares of Common Stock (as defined below) (the “
Warrants
”) and (2) 433,323,000 shares of common stock of the Company, par value $0.001 per share (“
Common Stock
”; such newly-issued shares of Common Stock (which number shall be subject to equitable adjustment for any stock dividend (including any dividend of securities convertible into or exchangeable for Common Stock), stock split (including a reverse stock split), stock combination, reclassification or similar transaction occurring on or after the date of this Agreement and prior to the Closing), the “
New Common Stock
”);
NOW, THEREFORE
, in consideration of the premises, and of the representations, warranties, covenants and agreements set forth herein, the parties agree as follows:
Article I
Closing
1.1
Issuance; Acceleration and Release
.
(a)
On the terms and subject to the conditions set forth in this Agreement, the Company agrees to issue to Commerce, on the Closing Date (as defined below), (a) Warrants to purchase 240,516,150 shares of Common Stock and (b) the New Common Stock.
(b)
The Parties agreed that in exchange for entering into this Agreement, that the US Government shall release as promptly as practicable, in accordance with applicable Law, $5,695,000,000.00 (“
Released Funds
”) in funding in respect of its obligations under the DFA, dated as of November 25, 2024 (other than with respect to Secure Enclave). Intel agrees that the US Government shall have no further obligations under the DFA (other than as required by law or with respect to Secure Enclave). Commerce agrees that to the maximum extent permissible under applicable Law, Intel’s obligations pursuant to the DFA
1
shall be considered discharged (other than with respect to Secure Enclave). The US Government shall also make the disbursements in respect of, and on the terms and conditions of, Secure Enclave and agrees to work with the Company to make appropriate amendments and modifications to the DFA to release Intel from certain of its obligations.
1.2
Closing
.
(a)
On the terms and subject to the conditions set forth in this Agreement, the closing of the issuance of the Warrants and the New Common Stock (the “
Closing
”) will take place on August 26, 2025 or as soon as practicable thereafter as the parties mutually agree (the date on which the Closing occurs, the “
Closing Date
”).
(b)
On the Closing Date, the Company will issue (x) to Commerce (i) a duly executed Warrant or Warrants to purchase 240,516,150 shares of Common Stock, as evidenced by one or more certificates dated the Closing Date and bearing appropriate legends as hereinafter provided for and in substantially the form attached hereto as
Annex A
, and (ii) the New Common Stock (other than the Escrowed Shares), together with evidence reasonably satisfactory to Commerce of the issuance of such New Common Stock in the name of Commerce in book entry form on the books of the Company’s transfer agent and (y) as set forth in Section 4.4(b), the Escrowed Shares into Escrow (such terms each as defined below). It is Commerce’s intention to release all the Released Funds on the Closing Date, to the extent any Released Funds are not released on the Closing Date, a number of shares equal to the amount of Released Funds not released divided by $20.00 shall not be delivered at the Closing Date but rather when such Released Funds are released.
(c)
On the Closing Date, the Company shall deliver to Commerce a certificate executed by the chief executive officer, president, executive vice president, chief financial officer, principal accounting officer, treasurer or controller confirming that the representations and warranties of the Company in this Agreement are true and correct with the same force and effect as though expressly made at and as of the Closing Date and the Company has complied with all agreements on its part to be performed or satisfied hereunder at or prior to such Closing.
(d)
On the Closing Date, the Company shall deliver to Commerce (i) such customary certificates of resolutions or other action, incumbency certificates and/or other certificates of the chief executive officer, president, executive vice president, chief financial officer, principal accounting officer, treasurer or controller as Commerce may require evidencing the identity, authority and capacity of each such officer thereof authorized to act as such officer in connection with this Agreement and (ii) customary resolutions or evidence of corporate authorization, secretary’s certificates and such other documents and certificates (including Organizational Documents and good standing certificates) as Commerce may reasonably request relating to the organization, existence and good standing of the Company and any other legal matters relating to the Company, this Agreement, the Warrants, the New Common Stock or the transactions contemplated hereby or thereby.

3.3
Sufficiency of Authorized Common Stock; Exchange Listing
. Nothing in this Section 3.3 shall preclude the Company from satisfying its obligations in respect of the exercise of the Warrants by delivery of shares of Common Stock which are held in the treasury of the Company. As soon as reasonably practicable on or after the Closing Date, the Company shall, at its expense, cause the New Common Stock and the Warrant Shares to be listed on the same national securities exchange on which the Common Stock is listed, subject to official notice of issuance, and shall maintain such listing for so long as any Common Stock is listed on such exchange. The Company will use commercially reasonable efforts to maintain the listing of Common Stock on such national securities exchange for so long as any New Common Stock, Warrants or Warrant Shares remain outstanding. Neither the Company nor any of its Subsidiaries shall take any action which would be reasonably expected to result in the delisting or suspension of the Common Stock on such exchange. The foregoing shall not preclude the Company from undertaking any transaction set forth in Section 4.3 subject to compliance with that provision.
