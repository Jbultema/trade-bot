## Publication limits

1. Chapter 13 may publish the absence of calculable asset- and claimant-level
   returns as a finding about economic observability.
2. River Bend and CCAC VIII may be described as partial observed priority maps,
   not bounded cash waterfalls or return models.
3. Saline may be described through distinct contract and regulatory recovery
   mechanisms. Those mechanisms must not be collapsed into an inferred
   first-loss order.
4. All nine scenario rows are governed counterfactual designs without assigned
   probabilities. A structured propagation description does not make an
   unobserved propagation path empirical.
5. The five hyperscaler attribution methods are a coverage/readiness matrix.
   They do not produce AI-specific accounting or economic returns.
6. No approximate bridge, commitment, contract headline, or contingent support
   amount may be summed into a return denominator without overlap and funding-
   state reconciliation.

## Remaining evidence gap

No reviewed project or company currently supplies the matched price, productive
utilization, operating-cost, maintenance/replacement-capital, cash-conversion,
claimant-waterfall, recovery, and terminal-value evidence required for the
requested return outputs. Chapter 13 therefore explains precisely what can and
cannot be measured; it does not fill those gaps with point estimates.
